About me

I build websites for home-service businesses like plumbers and roofers, and I build AI and automation tools. I do each job from start to finish, from the first layout to the live site.

I'm a Computer Science & Engineering graduate from AIUB. I've designed and built 64 complete websites for real service businesses in plumbing, roofing, HVAC and automotive. Most of them run on a reusable template system I wrote, so I don't rebuild the same groundwork for each of the 18 industries I cover.

On the AI side, I built a team of AI agents that each do one step, from research and script to build, test and deploy. Together they turn a topic into a finished short video with voice and captions. I also built and run my own lead system in Python and Airtable. It finds businesses to contact and scores them.

I also spent three months as a networking intern at Trust Bank, and my thesis was machine-learning research. Both taught me what goes on underneath the browser, which changes how I look at a slow page.
